Radar rasters for the notebook, made from the exports of AhmadAmmar/sar-reading-manual (src/01_london_basics.py,
src/02_sindh_flood.py), compressed; coarser grids were averaged in linear power and converted back to dB.
- london_mean_vv_10m.tif, london_single_vv_10m.tif: VV dB, 10 m, EPSG:27700 (mean of 2024; one scene 7 Jan 2024)
- london_2024_mean_80m.tif: VV, VH, VV-VH (dB), 2024 mean, 80 m, EPSG:27700
- sindh_flood_200m.tif: VV dB 25 Aug-15 Sep 2021, same weeks 2022, JRC water occurrence (%), 200 m, UTM 42N
Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2021-2024; JRC Global Surface Water 1.4.
- indus_gedi_footprints.csv: GEDI L2A good-quality shots over Indus Delta mangroves 2019-2023 (NASA/USGS, public),
  with Sentinel-1, PALSAR-2, Copernicus GLO-30, FABDEM and Sentinel-2 NDVI sampled at each footprint
  (from AhmadAmmar/gedi-mangroves outputs/footprints_l2a.csv; duplicates removed)
- karachi_insar_crop.npz: 68 unwrapped Sentinel-1 interferograms (ASF HyP3 INSAR_GAMMA, path 42 ascending, 2023-2024),
  coherence, baselines and the MintPy velocity, cropped to east Karachi and averaged to 160 m
  (commons-city src/karachi/15-17). Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2023-2024, processed by ASF.
