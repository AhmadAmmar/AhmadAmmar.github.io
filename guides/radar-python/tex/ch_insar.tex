\chapter{Is Karachi sinking? InSAR time series}\label{ch:insar}
The radar echo carries a phase as well as a strength. Differencing the phase of two passes measures ground motion to millimetres. Here we build a two-year time series of Karachi's ground from 68 interferograms, reproduce the core of MintPy in numpy, and find a bowl of subsidence under Korangi and Landhi. Notebook: \texttt{insar.ipynb}. Making a DEM from one InSAR pair is covered in the Karakoram hazard tutorial.
