How do people who publish course notes, guides, books and papers on the web keep editing them after the first release? This guide looks at what they write in, which editors they use, where the pages are built, how a change reaches readers, and follows each workflow step by step from opening the file to the published page. It starts with a colleague's teaching site, compares well-known open projects whose repositories anyone can read, sets our own LaTeX-based stack beside them, and ends with what is worth adopting and how to edit our documents today.
