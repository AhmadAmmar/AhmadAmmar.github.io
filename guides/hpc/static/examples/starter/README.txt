HPC starter kit: seven small exercises. Instructions: https://ahmadammar.github.io/teaching/hpc/first-jobs/

All scripts use only Python's standard library, so there is nothing to install.

Data
data/armaghdata.txt: Armagh Observatory monthly records. Source: Met Office historic station data,
  https://www.metoffice.gov.uk/research/climate/maps-and-data/historic-station-data
  Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0.
data/cities_natural_earth.csv: populated places from Natural Earth (public domain), https://www.naturalearthdata.com
Satellite tiles in exercise 7 are synthetic: 07_make_tiles.py invents them.
