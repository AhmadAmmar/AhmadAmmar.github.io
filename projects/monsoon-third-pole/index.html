Monsoon and Third Pole climate

Earth Engine work on the South Asian monsoon and on snow, water and basins across the Third Pole.

This work builds regional climate records from sea surface temperature, rainfall and temperature data, and basin and mountain-range records of snow across the Third Pole. It grew out of an earlier study of El Niño and La Niña over the Indian monsoon region. The working scripts are public.
