Marxian Economics 101: Understanding Political Economy for Socialist Struggle in the 21st Century

An introduction to political economy as a tool for organising. It begins with dialectical and historical materialism, works through commodities and value, labour-power and surplus value, the circuits of capital, the rate of profit and crisis, then applies these ideas to reformism, financialisation and real estate, social democracy, imperialism and unequal exchange, social reproduction and unpaid labour, and technology and the future of work.
