Profile

Doctoral researcher at Ulster University developing open, transferable methods for estimating the energy efficiency of buildings from Earth observation and geospatial data. A decade of training and work in geo-informatics, remote sensing and spatial data science, across LiDAR forest structure, deep-learning image segmentation, air quality, hydrology, weather data engineering and administrative data research. Postgraduate teaching assistant in physical geography, environmental science and GIS, and author of open study guides and an interactive atlas for atmospheric science. Interests span geocomputation, Earth observation, urbanism, climate change, health geography and spatial epidemiology.

Sep 2019 – Aug 2023

MS Remote Sensing and Geo-Information Science

Geospatial Research and Education Lab (GREL), Institute of Space Technology (IST), Islamabad · CGPA 3.80/4.00, merit scholarship

Dissertation: Assessing Global Ecosystem Dynamics Investigation (GEDI) LiDAR data for mangrove canopy height and above-ground biomass density mapping in the Indus Delta, Pakistan. Random forest models in Google Earth Engine predicted GEDI L2A RH98 canopy height and L4B biomass density from features of ALOS PALSAR-2, Sentinel-1 SAR and Sentinel-2 imagery; the continuous surfaces were validated against field plots. Abstract.

Supervisor: Dr Hammad Gilani, remote sensing and GIS researcher, International Water Management Institute (IWMI).

Coursework: advanced remote sensing and digital image processing; spatial thinking for monitoring and mapping natural resources; advanced GIS; advanced research methods; advanced programming and customising GIS; environmental sciences; big geospatial data analysis with Google Earth Engine; hydrometeorology.

Sep 2015 – Aug 2019

BS (Hons) Geo-Informatics

Institute of Geo-Information and Earth Observation (IGEO), PMAS Arid Agriculture University Rawalpindi · CGPA 3.92/4.00, gold medal and merit scholarship

Final-year project: Automated extraction of urban features from a fusion of multisource satellite datasets using deep learning. An end-to-end pipeline in Keras and fastai compared U-Net, SSD and Mask R-CNN; U-Net was the most suitable for the fused data, with an F1 score of 0.89. Code and dataset.

Supervisor: Dr Hasan Ali Baig, Assistant Professor, UAAR.

Coursework: GIS; remote sensing; physical and human geography; earth sciences; physics; statistics; calculus; linear algebra; discrete mathematics; object-oriented programming; geodatabase systems; digital image processing; GIS programming; web GIS development; computer graphics; CAD; cartography; surveying; navigation systems; photogrammetry; geodesy; soil mechanics; landscape modelling; environmental engineering; hydrology; agriculture; weather analysis and forecasting; spatial decision support systems; spatial data infrastructure; natural resource management.

Research

2024 – present

Building energy efficiency from Earth observation

Doctoral project, Ulster University · Greater London and Belfast
  • Predicting ordinal EPC bands (A–G) from open satellite, map and climate data, with London as the development area and Belfast as the transfer test.
  • Building reproducible building-level databases that link certificates, addresses and footprints through UPRNs.
  • Multi-year Earth Engine composites (Sentinel-1 and 2, Landsat, MODIS, VIIRS, ERA5) and local Python and PyQGIS processing into point and zonal features; tests of year-to-year feature stability.
  • Random forest and gradient-boosted baselines with feature selection and SHAP interpretation; spatial and temporal generalisation as a core test.
