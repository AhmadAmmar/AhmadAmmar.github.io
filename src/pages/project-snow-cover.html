Snow cover in the Upper Indus

Automated MODIS snow-cover mapping across the Indus headwaters, now being rebuilt at a wider scale.

The first version downloaded MODIS scenes in bulk and used ArcPy to calculate snow-covered area for every eight-day period from 2000 to 2019.

A new study is being built in Google Earth Engine and Python, covering sub-basins, the Hindu Kush, Karakoram and Himalaya, and the wider Third Pole, with explicit treatment of cloud and links to climate. Results will follow publication.
