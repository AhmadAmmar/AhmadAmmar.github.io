Teaching · hands-on notebooks

Know your study area

Eight notebooks for researchers who know a place through people, archives, texts or fieldwork and want maps that help them think and argue: ethnographers, historians, sociologists, political scientists, and scholars of gender, labour, religion or literature. The worked examples are Quetta and the province of Balochistan. No GIS background is assumed and nothing needs installing: open any notebook in Google Colab and press Shift + Enter.

Everything in one notebook

All eight chapters joined in order, with linked contents and chapter-to-chapter navigation: one file to open, run or download.

Before you start

Run notebooks 01 and 02 first in the same Colab session (or keep their data folder on Google Drive): later notebooks read the files they make. Field-data examples are invented. Read notebook 07 before collecting or publishing anything about people.
