The Atmosphere: a book worked step by step

One continuous book in three parts. Every question, table, graph, map and worked answer sits together on the page, and the equations are tested Python functions. The questions and data grew out of the EGM305 practicals and are reused with the permission of the module staff.

Reading

The module reading schedule follows two textbooks: Lutgens, Tarbuck and Herman, The Atmosphere: An Introduction to Meteorology, and Ahrens and Henson, Meteorology Today; the book's reading section gives more. Free, open alternatives:

  • Stull, R. (2017) Practical Meteorology: An Algebra-based Survey of Atmospheric Science. University of British Columbia. Free online edition.
  • The Met Office: Learn about weather pages, and its surface pressure charts for practising map analysis on today's weather.
